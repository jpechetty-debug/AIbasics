1️⃣ Curriculum Integrity & Coverage (Final Pass)

Even after quality/continuity checks, verify:

✅ Every module has:

Lessons

At least one exercise

At least one assessment (quiz/assignment/project)

✅ No orphan pages (content not linked in navigation)

✅ No empty or placeholder files

✅ Capstone clearly integrates multiple prior modules

✅ Learning objectives actually appear in lessons and assessments

👉 Outcome: Curriculum is complete, intentional, and closed-loop.

2️⃣ Assessment Alignment & Rigor

Check assessments as a system, not individually:

Do quizzes test what was just taught (not future topics)?

Do assignments require application, not repetition?

Does difficulty increase gradually across weeks?

Are explanations present for:

Correct answers

Incorrect answers

Are there enough assessments to justify a paid/professional course?

👉 Outcome: Assessments validate real understanding, not memorization.

3️⃣ UI–Content Fit (Critical but Often Missed)

Verify that good content is not degraded by UI:

Long lessons are readable (line width, spacing, headings)

Tables and diagrams render correctly on:

Desktop

Mobile

Code blocks, exercises, and callouts stand out visually

Quizzes are:

Easy to interact with

Clearly show feedback

Navigation never hides or buries important content

👉 Outcome: Content quality survives delivery.

4️⃣ Navigation & Learning Flow (User Perspective)

Walk through the course as a learner:

Can a user always answer:

“Where am I?”

“What should I do next?”

Is “Next / Previous” always logical?

Is progress visible and trustworthy?

Can learners resume after leaving?

👉 Outcome: Zero confusion during learning.

5️⃣ Progress Tracking Accuracy

Progress tracking must be correct and meaningful:

Lesson completion only when viewed fully (or explicitly marked)

Quiz completion reflects:

Attempt

Score

Assignments reflect:

Submitted

Pending review

Module progress matches reality

👉 Outcome: Learners trust the system.

6️⃣ Content Consistency & Tone

Even high-quality content can feel fragmented:

Terminology consistency (same term = same meaning)

Tone consistency (mentor-like, not mixed styles)

Diagram style consistency

No sudden changes in depth or verbosity

👉 Outcome: Course feels authored, not assembled.

7️⃣ Enterprise Credibility Check

Ask: Would a senior network admin respect this?

Are examples realistic?

Are claims conservative and defensible?

No hype or marketing fluff

Clear boundaries of what AI can and cannot do

No oversimplifications that insult expertise

👉 Outcome: Enterprise trust.

8️⃣ Accessibility & Inclusivity

Must-haves for professional platforms:

Keyboard navigation works everywhere

Color contrast sufficient

Semantic HTML used correctly

Diagrams explained in text

No “visual-only” explanations

👉 Outcome: Professionally accessible.

9️⃣ Performance & Reliability

Especially important for text-heavy platforms:

Pages load fast

No blocking scripts

Markdown rendering is safe and sanitized

Missing files handled gracefully

No broken links or crashes

👉 Outcome: Stable, production-ready platform.

🔟 Final Market Differentiation Check

This answers: “Why is this the best?”

Does the course:

Respect the learner’s intelligence?

Teach practical judgment, not just concepts?

Avoid unnecessary content?

Is it clearly better than:

Generic AI courses

Video-heavy fluff courses

Non-IT-focused AI training?

👉 Outcome: Clear market positioning.

✔ Content is deep enough

✔ Flow is logical

✔ UI supports learning

✔ Assessments are fair and rigorous

✔ Progress tracking is accurate

✔ Enterprise audience is respected

✔ No videos needed

✔ Diagrams add clarity

✔ Platform feels premium
