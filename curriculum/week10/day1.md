# AI Skills Employers are Looking For

Understand the changing landscape of the IT job market and how to position yourself as an AI-enabled professional.

## Objective
Identify the top 3 AI competencies currently in demand for network and systems roles.

## Content
- **The Shift from "Running" to "Orchestrating"**: How AI changes the sysadmin role.
- **Top Competencies**: Prompt Engineering, AI Governance, and RAG Implementation.
- **Showcasing Your Work**: Building a portfolio of AI-driven automations.
- **Continuous Learning**: Keeping up with the 6-month AI innovation cycle.

## Practical Exercise
Update your LinkedIn "Skills" section or resume to reflect the 10 weeks of AI training you have completed. Focus on specific tools and methodologies (e.g., "Multi-Agent System Design," "Advanced Prompt Patterns").
