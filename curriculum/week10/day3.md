# Ethics, Bias, and Responsible AI

Learn the critical frameworks for implementing AI safely and ethically in a corporate environment.

## Objective
Identify potential risks in AI outputs and learn how to mitigate them.

## Content
- **Understanding Bias**: Where it comes from in training data.
- **Hallucinations**: Why LLMs lie and how to fact-check them.
- **Data Privacy**: Protecting company IP when using public AI tools.
- **Human-in-the-Loop**: Why AI should assist, not replace, judgment.

## Practical Exercise
Take an AI-generated network security policy and perform a "Bias Audit". Identify 3 areas where the AI might have made assumptions that could be risky or incorrect for your specific environment.
