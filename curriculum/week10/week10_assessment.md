# Week 10 Assessment: Career & Responsible AI

This final assessment covers the strategic, ethical, and security aspects of AI in the workplace.

## Section 1: Career Strategy

### Question 1
**What does it mean to be an "Augmented Professional"?**

A) To have a computer chip implanted.
B) To use AI to automate "doing" tasks while focusing on direction, strategy, and verification.
C) To replace your entire team with AI bots.
D) To only work 15 minutes a day.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
Augmentation is about enhancing human capability with AI assistance.

---

### Question 2
**Which task is considered "AI-Resistant"?**

A) Writing a standard Python script.
B) Summarizing a long PDF.
C) Managing complex human conflicts and cultural change.
D) Generating an FAQ list.

**Correct Answer: C**

**Feedback:**
Human-centric leadership remains the hardest skill for AI to replicate.

## Section 2: Ethics & Responsibility

### Question 3
**What is the "Human-in-the-Loop" (HITL) principle?**

A) Humans should always type faster than the AI.
B) Critical AI outputs must be reviewed and approved by a human before action.
C) Humans should only use AI in circles.
D) AI should be allowed to run the company alone.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
HITL is essential for safety, accountability, and quality control.

---

### Question 4
**Why is "Explainability" important in AI decisions?**

A) To make the AI sound smarter.
B) To provide a reasoning that can be audited for fairness and compliance.
C) To make the code longer.
D) It isn't important; we should just trust the AI.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
In a professional setting, we must be able to justify why a decision was made.

## Section 3: Security & Privacy

### Question 5
**What is the most effective way to prevent "Data Leakage" to public AI models?**

A) Unplug the internet.
B) Use enterprise-grade AI services with non-training guarantees and sanitize all data.
C) Trust that the AI will keep secrets.
D) Change your password every day.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
Enterprise contracts and data sanitization are the primary defenses against data leakage.

---

### Question 6
**What is a "Prompt Injection" attack?**

A) Injecting a virus into the AI's power supply.
B) Using crafted input to trick the AI into bypassing its safety rules or leaking data.
C) Asking the same question 100 times.
D) Sending a prompt via email.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
Prompt injection is a significant security vulnerability in AI-integrated applications.

## Section 4: Leadership & Future

### Question 7
**How should a leader address "AI Anxiety" in their team?**

A) By firing the most worried people.
B) By shifting the narrative from "Replacement" to "Augmentation" and providing psychological safety.
C) By telling them that AI isn't real.
D) By making everyone use AI for 12 hours a day.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
Supportive leadership is key to successful AI adoption.

---

### Question 8
**What is the final step in the "AI Mastery Roadmap"?**

A) Retirement.
B) Strategic leadership and mentoring others.
C) Deleting your prompt library.
D) Buying more AI stock.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
True mastery involves not just using the technology, but leading others in its effective and ethical application.
