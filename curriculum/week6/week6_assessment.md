# Week 6 - Weekly Interactive Assessment

## Low-Code AI Apps Quiz

**Instructions:**
- 15 questions covering Chatbots, RAG, and Low-Code Concepts.
- Aim for 70% or higher.

**Scoring Guide:**
- 13-15: AI Architect 🏗️
- 10-12: Bot Builder 🤖
- <10: Review Week 6 📄

---

### Question 1 (Concepts)
**What is the primary benefit of "Low-Code" AI for a total beginner?**

A) It requires deep programming knowledge first.
B) It allows building tools (chatbots/search) visually without writing full application code.
C) It is always slower to build.
D) It's only useful for games.

**Correct Answer:** B

**Feedback:**
- **B) ✓ Correct!** Rapid, accessible tool creation is the key benefit.

---

### Question 2 (RAG)
**RAG solves which major limitation of general AI models?**

A) Lack of logic.
B) Lack of access to your private or personal data (beyond their training cutoff).
C) Being too fast.
D) Being too cheap.

**Correct Answer:** B

**Feedback:**
- **B) ✓ Correct!** It bridges the gap between the general model and your own specific data.

---

### Question 3 (Configuration)
**In a Custom Bot, the "System Prompt" is used to:**

A) Define the Persona, Tone, and Rules.
B) Log the user in.
C) Store the uploaded files.
D) Change the background color.

**Correct Answer:** A

**Feedback:**
- **A) ✓ Correct!** It is the "personality and rulebook" of the bot.

---

### Question 4 (Vector DB)
**A Vector Database stores data as:**

A) Plain keywords only.
B) Mathematical Embeddings (Numbers) representing meaning.
C) Only images.
D) Only audio.

**Correct Answer:** B

**Feedback:**
- **B) ✓ Correct!** This enables searching by meaning ("similar to this idea"), not just exact word matching.

---

### Question 5 (Chunking)
**When preparing a document for RAG, "Chunking" means:**

A) Deleting pages you don't like.
B) Splitting the text into smaller segments to fit the context window.
C) Compressing the file size.
D) Translating it into another language.

**Correct Answer:** B

**Feedback:**
- **B) ✓ Correct!** AI models ingest chunks, not whole libraries at once.

---

### Question 6 (Guardrails)
**"Do not answer questions about topics unrelated to our store." This is an example of:**

A) A Guardrail / Constraint.
B) A Hallucination.
C) A Temperature setting.
D) A Bug.

**Correct Answer:** A

**Feedback:**
- **A) ✓ Correct!** Instructions that restrict the bot's behavior to what's appropriate.

---

### Question 7 (Retrieval)
**If a RAG system retrieves the wrong document context, the AI's answer will likely be:**

A) Correct anyway, somehow.
B) Wrong or irrelevant (a hallucination based on bad context).
C) Perfect.
D) Faster than usual.

**Correct Answer:** B

**Feedback:**
- **B) ✓ Correct!** Garbage in (bad retrieval) leads to garbage out (bad generation).

---

### Question 8 (Visuals)
**In a visual flow builder, a "Node" represents:**

A) A step in the process (e.g., a document loader, an AI model call).
B) A specific user.
C) An error message.
D) Just a decorative line.

**Correct Answer:** A

**Feedback:**
- **A) ✓ Correct!** Nodes are the functional building blocks of the flow.

---

### Question 9 (Integration)
**Can a Low-Code RAG bot be deployed into a team chat platform like Slack or Teams?**

A) No, never.
B) Yes, especially using enterprise-focused tools built for that purpose.
C) Only by sending emails.
D) It's technically impossible.

**Correct Answer:** B

**Feedback:**
- **B) ✓ Correct!** Team/enterprise integration is a common and valuable feature.

---

### Question 10 (Temperature)
**For a bot that needs to give strict, factual customer support answers, the Temperature should be:**

A) High (0.9).
B) Low (0.0 - 0.2).
C) Medium (0.5).
D) It doesn't matter at all.

**Correct Answer:** B

**Feedback:**
- **B) ✓ Correct!** Low temperature keeps answers consistent and factual.

---

### Question 11 (Citations)
**Why are citations important in a policy-answering RAG bot?**

A) They prove the answer came from an actual, real source document.
B) They just look nice.
C) They reduce token usage.
D) They are entirely optional and never useful.

**Correct Answer:** A

**Feedback:**
- **A) ✓ Correct!** Citations let you verify the answer instead of blindly trusting it.

---

### Question 12 (Use Case)
**Which is a good use case for a Custom Bot (without RAG)?**

A) Searching a 500-page manual for a specific fact.
B) A "Persona" bot that explains concepts in a fun, consistent style (like a pirate).
C) Calculating precise tax figures.
D) Storing large files.

**Correct Answer:** B

**Feedback:**
- **B) ✓ Correct!** Stylistic, persona-based transformations don't require external data lookup.

---

### Question 13 (Use Case)
**Which is a good use case for RAG?**

A) "Write a poem."
B) "What is the return policy in our 2026 store handbook?"
C) "Translate this sentence."
D) "Hello there."

**Correct Answer:** B

**Feedback:**
- **B) ✓ Correct!** Fact retrieval from a specific, real document is exactly what RAG is for.

---

### Question 14 (Maintenance)
**If the "Return Policy" changes, you should:**

A) Replace the entire AI model.
B) Update the document in the Knowledge Base.
C) Do nothing and hope it updates itself.
D) Rewrite unrelated code.

**Correct Answer:** B

**Feedback:**
- **B) ✓ Correct!** The underlying model stays the same; only the data source needs updating.

B) An e-book.

**Feedback:**
