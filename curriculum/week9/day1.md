# Workplace Productivity Hacks

Learn how to use Generative AI to boost your daily efficiency and get more done in less time.

## Objective
Identify 5 core areas where AI can immediately save you 2+ hours per day.

## Content
- **Meeting Mastery**: Summarizing transcripts and extracting action items.
- **Schedule Optimization**: Using AI to block time and prioritize tasks.
- **Information Synthesis**: Rapidly digesting long documents and research.
- **Workflow Automation**: Connecting AI to your calendar and tasks.

## Practical Exercise
Take a transcript from a recent 30-minute meeting and use AI to generate:
1. A 3-sentence summary.
2. A list of all stakeholders mentioned.
3. A table of action items with owners and deadlines.
