# AI-Powered Data Analysis & Reporting

Transform raw data into professional reports and actionable insights using AI analysis tools.

## Objective
Learn how to use AI as a virtual data scientist for your IT metrics.

## Content
- **Pattern Recognition**: Identifying anomalies in logs and performance data.
- **Data Transformation**: Cleaning and reformatting complex datasets with natural language.
- **Insight Generation**: Asking "Why" and "What if" questions of your data.
- **Professional Reporting**: Converting analysis into executive-ready summaries.

## Practical Exercise
Provide a CSV of network latency logs to the AI and ask it to:
1. Identify the peak congestion periods.
2. Correlate latency with specific server nodes.
3. Generate a draft executive report recommending infrastructure upgrades based on the findings.
