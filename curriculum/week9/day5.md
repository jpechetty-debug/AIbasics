# AI Solutions Implementation Lab

The final practical session for Week 9 - focusing on building your personal "AI Productivity Suite".

## Objective
Finalize and deploy a set of 5 automated AI prompts that you will use in your work starting Monday.

## Lab Tasks
- **Refinement**: Take your best prompts from the week and use the "Critic Pattern" to improve them.
- **Organization**: Store your prompts in a structured library or low-code tool.
- **Testing**: Run your prompts against real (anonymized) work data.
- **Documentation**: Write a short guide for your teammates on how to use these tools.

## Lab Assessment
Submit your "AI Productivity Suite" (a document containing your 5 refined prompts and their use cases) for peer review.
