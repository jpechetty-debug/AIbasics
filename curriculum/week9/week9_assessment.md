# Week 9 Assessment: Professional AI Solutions

This assessment covers the core concepts and practical applications of using AI for professional productivity, data analysis, and communication.

## Section 1: Core Frameworks

### Question 1
**In the C.A.T. framework, what does the 'T' stand for?**

A) Training.
B) Translation.
C) Transformation.
D) Telemetry.

**Correct Answer: C**

**Feedback:**
C.A.T. stands for Capture, Analyze, and Transform.

---

### Question 2
**Which technique involves using AI to identify if you were actually needed in a recorded meeting?**

A) The Ghost Protocol.
B) The Shadow Attendee.
C) The Proxy Pattern.
D) The Echo Analysis.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
The Shadow Attendee technique helps optimize your schedule by evaluating meeting necessity.

## Section 2: Data & Analysis

### Question 3
**When using AI for data analysis, what is "Anomaly Detection"?**

A) Writing new records manually.
B) Identifying data points that deviate from the standard or expected pattern.
C) Deleting all records older than 30 days.
D) Calculating a simple average.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
AI is excellent at spotting patterns and, more importantly, identifying things that don't fit those patterns.

---

### Question 4
**Why might you use AI for "ETL" (Extract, Transform, Load) tasks?**

A) It can parse unstructured data without complex formulas or scripts.
B) It requires no electricity to run.
C) It replaces the need for a spreadsheet entirely.
D) It only works with Excel files.

**Correct Answer: A**

**Feedback:**
AI's ability to handle natural language and varied formats makes it ideal for cleaning and transforming inconsistent data.

## Section 3: Professional Communication

### Question 5
**What is "Tone Mapping" in the context of AI communication?**

A) Changing the font of an email.
B) Adjusting the language and style to match the target audience (e.g., technical vs. executive).
C) Translating an email into a different language.
D) Predicting when the recipient will reply.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
Matching your tone to the audience is key to ensuring your message is understood and respected.

---

### Question 6
**How can AI assist with creating a "Post-Mortem" or incident summary?**

A) By omitting what actually caused the problem.
B) By summarizing messy notes into a readable narrative for different stakeholders.
C) By fixing the underlying problem automatically.
D) By deleting the evidence of what happened.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
AI acts as a bridge, translating messy notes and facts into clear, actionable summaries for various audiences.

## Section 4: Solution Implementation

### Question 7
**Why is it important to maintain a "Personal Prompt Library"?**

A) To show off to your boss.
B) To ensure you have consistent, high-quality results for recurring tasks.
C) Because prompts expire every 24 hours.
D) It's required by law.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
A structured library saves time and ensures that once you've perfected a prompt, you can use it instantly next time.

---

### Question 8
**What is the "Critic Pattern" used for?**

A) To find bugs in your code.
B) To iteratively improve the quality and constraints of your AI prompts.
C) To leave bad reviews on software products.
D) To replace the need for human oversight.

**Correct Answer: B**

**Feedback:**
Using the AI to critique your own prompts is one of the fastest ways to improve your skill as a prompt engineer.
